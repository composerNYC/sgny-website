LAW OF ADORATION — DRAWING CLOSER TO GOD is the theme of Issue 25 of the Revue Spirite – Spiritist Review.

This issue invites readers to reflect on worship, the elevation of thought, the presence of God, and moral progress, in the light of Spiritism, science, philosophy, and ethics. Drawing on the teachings of Allan Kardec, the authors question established concepts and explore the relationship of human beings with themselves, with others, and with God, emphasizing moral transformation as the path toward drawing closer to divine love, justice, and perfection.
