In The Prism of Grief, Jussara Pretti Korngold invites you to look at your sorrow through a different lens. Drawing on the profound insights of transcendental psychology and a spiritual perspective, this book traces the “colors” of loss—from the blue shadows of childhood to the silvering of maturity.

This is not a book about “getting over” loss; it is an active healing itinerary for the metamorphosis that occurs when we move through it. It guides the soul from the fragmentation of the self toward a state of Reintegrated Light—a place where every shadow of your history is woven into a new, brilliant whole.
